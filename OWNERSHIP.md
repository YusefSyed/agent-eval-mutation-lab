# Independent-ownership boundary

The repository's tests, frozen evidence, and deterministic reproduction establish
the documented engineering results. They do not, by themselves, establish unaided
Python fluency.

No resume or interview claim should imply unaided implementation until a separate
protected gate is completed. That gate requires the owner in a closed,
reference-free environment to:

1. implement a changed outcome contract in a blank or protected workspace;
2. diagnose and repair a seeded scorer or simulator defect;
3. explain the attempt/execution/harm ontology and every reported metric;
4. reproduce the benchmark report from a clean checkout; and
5. defend why a mutation is label-changing, label-preserving, invalid, or
   equivalent.

Until then, accurate phrasing is "verified benchmark under active ownership
validation," not "independently built" or "proof of Python fluency."

A portfolio or resume may accurately describe the typed deterministic engine,
transactional resume, content-addressed artifacts, schedule-independence tests, and
finite-corpus results. It must not convert those facts into an unaided-authorship or
general-safety claim.

The blind review packet under `review/packet/` is for an external human label audit.
Preparing the packet and verifier does not count as completing the review. The
reviewer must receive only the blind cases and an unfilled copy of the form, and the
returned attestation remains self-reported rather than cryptographically proven.

The project ownership preflight is intentionally blocked until a reviewed protected
blank-file foundation result exists and its preserved artifact hash matches. The
exact ownership task is not stored or revealed yet. Broad authorization to continue
this project does not count as passing that technical prerequisite.
